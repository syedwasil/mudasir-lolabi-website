About the Chairman

Serving the people at the grassroots

Er. Mudasir Lolabi serves as the Chairman of the Rajiv Gandhi Panchayati Raj Organization (RGPRS) for Jammu & Kashmir, under the Indian National Congress. Appointed by the party high command, he leads RGPRS efforts to strengthen local self-governance and democratic participation across the region.

An engineer by profession, he brings technical clarity and public commitment to his role — representing Panchayati Raj bodies, engaging with youth leaders, and standing with citizens on issues that matter at the village, block, and district level.

His leadership reflects the Congress vision of empowering every village through vibrant Panchayati Raj — ensuring democracy reaches the last mile of Jammu & Kashmir.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead RGPRS across Jammu & Kashmir
  • Strengthen Panchayati Raj institutions

About RGPRS

Rajiv Gandhi Panchayati Raj Organization

The Rajiv Gandhi Panchayati Raj Organization (RGPRS) is the Indian National Congress platform dedicated to strengthening Panchayati Raj — the foundation of grassroots democracy envisioned by Rajiv Gandhi through the 73rd Constitutional Amendment.

As Chairman for Jammu & Kashmir, Er. Mudasir Lolabi coordinates RGPRS outreach with elected Panchayat representatives, block and district bodies, youth wings, and Congress workers to ensure local voices shape policy and development priorities.

  • Capacity building for Panchayat leaders and PRI representatives
  • Advocacy for funds, functions, and functionaries at the village level
  • Coordination with INC J&K on civic issues and public welfare
  • Youth engagement in democratic institutions and local governance

73rd Amendment — at a glance

Constitutional status for Panchayats, regular elections, reserved seats for women and SC/ST communities, and devolution of powers — the pillars of vibrant local self-government in India.
